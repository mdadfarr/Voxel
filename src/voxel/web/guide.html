How to use Voxel

Four steps and a few minutes. The gallery below shows what past builds cost.

  1. 1

    Paste your key

    Your Anthropic key pays for the build. It is used once and never saved.

  2. 2

    Add your sets

    Search by name or number. Voxel builds only with the bricks in those sets.

  3. 3

    Say what to build

    One line works. More detail (colors, features, pose) gives you more control.

  4. 4

    Download the manual

    You get a picture, a review, and an .ldr manual you can open in any LDraw viewer.
